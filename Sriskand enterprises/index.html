UPVC (Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride) is a rigid, durable polymer specifically engineered for window and door profiles. Unlike traditional wood or standard metals, it does not rot, corrode, flake, or peel, and requires no periodic repainting or chemical treatments.

UPVC windows offer superior acoustic insulation (noise reduction), thermal efficiency to keep rooms cooler, termite-proof resistance, watertight joints against monsoon seepage, exceptional strength with galvanized steel reinforcement, and minimal maintenance needs over their long service life.
